Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who runs this site

This site is an independent guide for buyers of Japanese second-hand cars in Malta. It is run by JPMc. Contact: info@japanmaltacheck.info.

What we collect

The site has no accounts and no forms. We do not ask for or store your name, email address or any chassis number you look up. When you press a link to JEVIC, your browser goes straight to JEVIC's website; we are not involved in what you enter there.

What is stored on your device

Your language choice and the items you tick on the checklist are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device only. They are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads if you agree. Where required, you will be asked for your consent first and can change your choice later. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Other websites

This site links to other websites such as JEVIC, Transport Malta, GOV.UK and MaltaToday. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You can also complain to the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) in Malta.
